Facilitator's notes, as told. Source: the decision log, D-010, D-017 and D-020, and the mission.

- Workshop participants tell stories in conversations. A project lead describes the work as "scaled to human note taker and focusing on obsidian for 'coding' data from conversations with the workshop participants."
- Facilitators take the raw notes. The setting is Chatham House rule: the people in the data are groups, so no individual is identifiable.
- Participants are clustered into groups. Project lead: "stakeholders in the data model are stakeholder groups, essentially clustered down to fit in a cardinality parameter (set per instance)".
- Project lead on the raw notes: "facilitators call; its not currently built for attribution because we're using it chatham house rule settings, but extensions could support projects where attribution is desired property."
- First case of a concern conflict comes up: attribution versus anonymity. Project lead: "we just had our first case of a concern conflict ("attribution") vs ("anonymity"); those can be related by a disjointness axiom."
