Facilitator's notes, as told. Source: the decision log, D-017 to D-022. Project leads' words are quoted as they stand.

- Mission approved. First work packages (the ontology research and the toolchain spike) under way.
- A project lead shares a stakeholder conversation as intent information. New intent: "affordances may be better than propeties here". Acronym becomes SCAT.
- Project lead: "one thing i noticed is that affordances may be better than propeties here. that said, it makes our acronym scat. i think we might as well roll with it."
- Project lead: "rename everything. i hope i caught you earlier enough. if you are worried about drift you could revert to an earlier commit, make the change in the mission before it propogates, and start again."
- Project lead: "my intuition is we do a roll back and mission revision then kick off again"
- Work in flight stopped before anything merged.
- The integration branch reverted to the approved mission. History kept, no force-push.
- Earlier findings kept as tags, so they stay available and do not seed the restart.
- Mission revised as an amendment. The amendment approved by a project lead's merge.
- Work restarted clean from the revised mission.
- Project lead, looking back: "we rolled back because we had a false start but fortunately our toolchain has affordances that allowed us to catch ourselves, refine our mission and then when its approved start again clean"
- Concerns in the telling: staying faithful to intent, and avoiding drift.
- Affordances in the telling: a gated mission; halting before anything merges; reverting without losing history; tags that keep findings available without seeding the restart.
